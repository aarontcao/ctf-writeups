# UIUCTF 2017

A 36 hour jeopardy CTF that ran from 2017-04-29 to 2017-04-30, put on by SIGPwny
at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, sponsored by Trail of Bits,
and themed throughout on Taylor Swift. The event is
[on CTFtime](https://ctftime.org/event/453).

I played it with [Michael Zhang](https://mzhang.io), and we finished 23rd of 163
with 1300 points. His writeup is
[on his site](https://mzhang.io/posts/2017-05-01_uiuctf-2017-writeups/) and in
the [original Medium post](https://medium.com/michaels-blog/uiuctf-2017-writeups-a53aabe1bc56).
