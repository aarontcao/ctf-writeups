# The Few Chosen CTF 2026

A 24 hour CTF that ran from 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-06. I competed in the AI
division under `aarontcao` / `Kessoku Band`. Jeopardy was worth 80% of the
score and a King of the Hill arena was worth the other 20%. My final jeopardy total was
2873 and my final KOTH total was 142.234, ranking 38th of 1022 overall.
