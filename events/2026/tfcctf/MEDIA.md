# Media index: The Few Chosen CTF 2026
This index collects every walkthrough screenshot I took for the TFC CTF 2026 writeup. I wasn't super attentive though, and unfortunately some challenge instances had already expired by the time I remembered I wanted to take captures. In those cases, I tried to make sure the screenshots render some helpful recorded artifact instead. For a few local rebuilds I bake in a placeholder flag rather than the real one.

Complete means the screenshots walk through the challenge e2e from handout to flag. Partial means the set is e2e but at least one shot is a replay, a ledger render, or a local placeholder.
